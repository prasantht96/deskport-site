Delete your data

DeskPort (by Prasanth T) has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers. We do not collect your personal data, so there is nothing stored about you on our side that we could delete.

What is stored, and where

DeskPort keeps your settings (theme, window layouts, shortcuts, desktop arrangement) only on your phone. If you use the optional notification centre, notifications are held in memory only and are never written to storage.

How to delete it

  • Clear the app's data: Settings, Apps, DeskPort, Storage, Clear storage (or Clear data).
  • Uninstall DeskPort: this removes all of its data from your phone.
  • Stop notification access: turn it off in DeskPort settings or in Android's notification access settings.
  • Revoke Shizuku access: open Shizuku and remove DeskPort from the authorized apps.
